Our Mission

Create healthy, low-carbon, and equitable environments where every community leads its own sustainable future.

Scroll

Mission in Action

SCS Canada mobilizes researchers, students, and community partners to bridge the gap between visionary sustainability goals and the places we use every day. We pair evidence-based design with local wisdom so that climate solutions are accessible, culturally grounded, and economically just.

Collaborative research

Applying human-centred design to real-world buildings and campuses.

Community prototypes

Piloting scalable solutions that lower emissions while improving comfort.

Open knowledge

Sharing playbooks and toolkits that empower communities nationwide.

Three Areas of Focus

We align our work around people, place, and possibilities for a just transition.

Learning for a Sustainable Future

We collaborate with educators to design curricula, workshops, and experiential learning that build climate literacy and empower lifelong sustainable habits.

Energy Equity & Wellbeing

Our research translates into policies and retrofits that reduce energy burdens, enhance comfort, and ensure every household benefits from low-carbon systems.

Community-Driven Futures

We convene residents, unions, and industry partners to co-create sustainable neighbourhood plans grounded in cultural resilience and collective care.
